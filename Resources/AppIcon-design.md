# Markdown Studio 应用图标

使用内置 imagegen 生成；透明 PNG 母版为 AppIcon.png，macOS 图标为 AppIcon.icns。
运行 `./scripts/build-icon.sh` 重新生成全部标准尺寸（16–1024 像素），再运行 `./scripts/build-app.sh` 打包。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished macOS app icon for Markdown Studio, a quiet focused Markdown writing editor. Square 1024x1024 asset with actual transparent background outside the icon. Centered forest-green rounded-square tile, generous transparent outer margin about 70 pixels, subtle dimensional bevel and soft restrained shadow. On the tile a single warm ivory paper sheet with a folded upper right corner, a bold forest-green Markdown M mark centered on the paper and two short understated writing lines below it. A small elegant ivory and green fountain pen nib overlaps the lower right of the paper, suggesting editing. Front facing, extremely clean silhouettes, restrained tactile macOS craftsmanship, sophisticated and calm, highly readable at 32px. Palette deep forest green #3d7259, ivory #fffdf5, subtle sage highlights. No words, no extra letters, no border frame, no desktop mockup, no multiple options, no watermark. Only one icon, transparent surrounding canvas.
